# GUIDELINES.md - Usage guidelines

Guidance for working with this mod and with the addon packs and addon
mods built for it.

This mod is designed around extension through external files: models,
textures, sounds, and other data that **are not part of the mod itself
and are supplied by the user**. That structure makes disputes over the
rights to such data particularly easy to run into.

## 1. Handling rights when building an addon pack or addon mod

**Using models, textures, sounds, or other data that other people hold
the rights to, without the rights holder's permission, is prohibited.**

This applies equally to addon packs (data placed in
`tudursvehiclemod-addons/`) and to addon mods (data bundled in a jar),
and regardless of whether you publish/distribute it or only use it
privately - **following the original data's own terms comes first**.

This mod only provides the machinery to load and run data. It takes no
responsibility for the rights status of whatever you load into it.

---

## 2. Specific cases

### Permitted

- **Data you hold the rights to** — models, textures, and sounds you made
  yourself
- **Use within the terms** — models from a paid or free asset store, used
  within what that license allows
- **You have the rights holder's permission** — you contacted the author
  directly and they agreed to its use with this mod

### Not permitted

- **A model that doesn't permit derivative use** — where the distributor
  doesn't allow modification, redistribution, or use in another work
- **A model whose terms restrict where it may be used** — for example one
  marked "personal use only" or "usable only inside a specific game or
  software", used beyond that scope
- **Data of unknown origin or unknown authorship** — anything whose
  permission status you can't verify

**When in doubt, don't use it.**

## 3. Handling MCHeli addon packs

This mod can read and convert MCHeli-format data, but **the existence of
that feature does not mean MCHeli addon packs are free to use.**

An MCHeli addon pack **may not be used with this mod unless its own
author has explicitly permitted use outside MCHeli.**

Most MCHeli addon packs are published on the premise of being used in
MCHeli, and running them in a different mod isn't something their authors
anticipated. The conversion working technically and the conversion being
permitted are two separate questions.

If you want to use one, **ask that pack's own author.**

## 4. Trouble scenarios and how to think about them

### Case 1: converted without reading the distribution site's terms

You found an MCHeli vehicle pack, converted it for this mod, and
published a video. Later you discover the distribution page says "MCHeli
only, porting to other mods prohibited."

→ **This violates the guidelines.** Whether the conversion tool ran
successfully is irrelevant. Check the terms before using anything.

### Case 2: a model labelled "free to use"

You used a model from a model distribution site labelled "free to use."

→ **It depends.** Where the scope of "free" is vague, it isn't clear
whether it extends to use in a game mod or to redistribution. Read the
whole distribution page, and ask the author about anything unclear.

### Case 3: bought a paid asset and used it as a model

You bought a vehicle model from an asset store, bundled it into your own
addon pack, and distributed that pack for free.

→ **Usually a terms violation.** Paid assets commonly permit "the
purchaser incorporating it into their own work" while **prohibiting
redistribution of the model data itself**. Here you'd need to not bundle
the model, and instead have each user buy it themselves.

### Case 4: modelled a real vehicle yourself

You modelled a real-world tank from scratch, using it as reference, and
published that.

→ **Generally fine.** The rights to model data you made belong to you.
Note that including company logos or emblems raises separate trademark
considerations.

### Case 5: used a model a friend made

You used a model made by a friend, with their permission, in your addon
pack.

→ **Fine.** To avoid trouble later, **keep a record of that permission**
(a message, say). Adding a credit to the pack is also courteous.

### Case 6: used a pack that was once distributed but no longer is

You still had the data from a pack whose distribution has since ended, so
you used it.

→ **Not permitted.** Rights persist even after distribution stops. And if
you can't reach the author, you can't obtain permission either - so don't
use it.

## 6. Notes on depiction

**This mod is intended for entertainment.**

Given a subject matter of vehicles and weapons, it's easy for things made
with it to be read as connected to real-world politics, religion,
conflicts, or incidents. **Using this mod or its output to advance actual
political arguments, to criticize or defend particular groups, or to
address ongoing or historical conflicts and incidents is not what it's
for.**

### On reproduction itself

Reproducing a real (or formerly real) vehicle, aircraft, or ship, along
with its unit markings and paint scheme, is **generally fine**.
Historical accuracy and model-kit-style reproduction are a legitimate
part of enjoying this subject matter.

### When careful judgement is needed

Where care is needed is in **producing and publishing images or video
that depict particular forces actually fighting each other.**

In cases like the following, it's easy to be read as making a political
argument you didn't intend, or to hurt people connected to the events.

- Combinations that evoke an ongoing conflict
- Content reproducing an actual incident, accident, or battle
- Anything that could be read as an attack on a particular nation,
  ethnicity, or religion

None of this is outright prohibited. But **before publishing, please stop
and consider whether it might unnecessarily hurt someone, or be taken as
a political statement.**

Using fictional forces and fictional settings often avoids the problem
entirely.
